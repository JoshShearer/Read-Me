First release.

Share an article link or any text to Read Me and it reads it aloud with your phone's own offline voice. Trim what you don't want first, then listen with the screen off; your place is kept. Controls work from headphones, the lock screen and the notification. No network voices, no tracking, no account.

Also includes a local speech service for Obsidian plugins on the same phone (off by default; the Local TTS Reader plugin's support is in development).
