Read Me now plays the speech itself, so media keys, the lock screen and headset buttons control it with any voice engine. Notes read from Obsidian through the bridge keep going with the screen off (with the plugin's new version). With Continuous play on, the next item starts sooner and an open Reader follows it. Settings can switch back to the engine's default voice. TalkBack reads each paragraph's text, and an item that cannot be opened says so and offers Retry.
