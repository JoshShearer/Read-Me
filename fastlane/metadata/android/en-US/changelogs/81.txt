Reading a note from Obsidian with the screen off needs Local TTS Reader 0.3.0 or newer. With an older version, Android can stop Obsidian's audio a few seconds after Obsidian leaves the screen, so keep Obsidian open. Settings > Obsidian bridge now says so. New shares now go to the bottom of the list, and Move puts any unread item where you want it; your existing items are listed oldest first.
